Reeve in Portuguese, a quieter town center alert in the late game and a cleaner build panel.

## New
- **Language**: the hub, alerts, build panel, reports, tray menu and notifications are available in English and Portuguese (Brazil). Reeve follows the Windows display language by default; choose another in Settings.
- **Older matches on demand**: the match list shows the last 3 days. **Load more** at the end of the list brings the last week, then the last month, then every match.

## Improved
- **Build panel**: the step arrows sit centered above the steps, and the rally point moves to the right end of the row. The arrows now show in your clips and recordings, like the alert cards.
- **Town center idle** stops in Age IV and from 170 population, when not making villagers is your choice. Both limits are adjustable in Settings.
- **Build steps advance sooner**: a step is reached as soon as its villager count is read, instead of a few seconds later.
- The build panel no longer shows the total villager count, and a resource with no villagers shows "–".
- Starting Reeve shows a notification that it is running in the tray; clicking a notification opens the hub.

## Install
Use **Update now** in the hub, or download `Reeve-0.3.2-setup.exe` below and run it; it updates an existing install and keeps your matches and settings. Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unknown publisher, since the app is not code-signed yet: choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
