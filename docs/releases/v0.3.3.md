Villager production read right for every civilization, build tips in the panel, and builds that no longer get stuck.

## New
- **Build tips**: advice the author wrote between steps ("if you get raided...") shows in a card under the current step. Turn it off in Settings, Builds.
- **Villager icons per civilization**: civilizations with their own villager art (Gilded Villager of the Order of the Dragon, Jin Mounted Villager, Malians, Japanese and others) are recognized in the production queue when you follow a build of that civilization.

## Improved
- The build panel leaves a resource empty when the step puts no villagers there, instead of showing "–". To bring the icon and dash back, use **Show places without villagers** in Settings, Builds.
- Reeve checks for a new version when it starts and every time you open the hub, instead of once a day.

## Fixed
- **Town center idle** sounded while the town center was making villagers: a villager in the queue over the map was often missed. This affected every civilization, and the Order of the Dragon the most.
- Builds with tip steps stopped at the first tip for the rest of the match, and a build ending with an empty age step never finished.
- Tips imported from aoe4guides.com came without their text.
- The language setting cut its text short.

## Install
Use **Update now** in the hub, or download `Reeve-0.3.3-setup.exe` below and run it; it updates an existing install and keeps your matches and settings. Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unknown publisher, since the app is not code-signed yet: choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
