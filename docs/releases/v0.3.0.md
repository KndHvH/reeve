Build orders: follow a build from aoe4guides.com on the right of the screen, with every civilization supported.

## New
- **Build orders**: import a build from [aoe4guides.com](https://aoe4guides.com) by pasting its link in the hub's new Builds page. During a match, the right of the screen shows the previous, current and next step: the age, the planned time, the villagers and where they go (food, wood, gold, stone, building), and the step's notes with the site's icons.
- **The build follows your pace**: a step is reached when you have its villagers and its age, not at a fixed time. Villagers building houses or mines are estimated from your population.
- **Villager placement alert**: when your villagers are not where the step puts them, an alert says what to do, such as "Move 1 villager from wood to food" or "Put 2 villagers on gold".
- **Rally point card**: above the steps, the rally point icon and where the build puts it (sheep, wood, gold...).
- **Build picker in the game's menus**: a card on the right of the screen chooses the build for the next match, or None to show nothing.
- **Every civilization**: the hub and the reports show each civilization's flag. The first time you use a build of a civilization, Reeve downloads its units, buildings, technologies and icons from AoE4World.
- **Civilization of each match**: the match list and the report show the civilization of the build you followed, with its flag.
- **Builds stay up to date**: when Reeve starts, it updates the builds their author changed on aoe4guides.com (can be turned off).
- Reports show the build the match followed and the time spent off the build.

## Improved
- Build steps come in, leave and move with the same animations as the alert cards; the build leaves the screen 30 seconds after its last step.
- The previous step stays 10 seconds and the next step shows after 10 seconds on the current one; both can be changed in the Builds page, along with the size of the build panel in Settings, Appearance, where a preview shows it.
- The idle town center alert stops from 100 villagers on resources (adjustable in Settings).
- Restarting a match starts a new match record, and a pause of up to 10 minutes no longer splits a match in two.

## Install
Use **Update now** in the hub, or download `Reeve-0.3.0-setup.exe` below and run it; it updates an existing install and keeps your matches and settings. Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unknown publisher, since the app is not code-signed yet: choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
